Ryan, 2026-08-12, after I defended leaving ICD10CM's position 2 as \\w:

> "I feel like you're leaving the regexp unnecessarily permissive"
> "the specifity isn't just protection against other vocabularies with similar formats, but from
> other random words and text that we might be trying to extract or detect codes"

He was right and the defence was measuring the WRONG POPULATION. Extends the trellis in vfm-ges.

## THE MISTAKE, NAMED SO IT IS NOT REPEATED

Every false-positive number in this repo, including the ones measured earlier the same day, scored a
row against OTHER VOCABULARIES -- 'how many ICD9CM codes does the ICD10CM row accept?' That is a
real question and it is not the only one. These regexps are used to FIND codes in text, so the
adversary is also ordinary English.

Scored against ICD9CM alone, narrowing position 2 looked pointless:

    ICD9CM accepted, position 2 = \\w            1,357
    ICD9CM accepted, position 2 = digit|QA       1,357     <- ZERO improvement

Because the ICD-9 codes that survive are V## and E###, which are already letter+digit. That number
alone says 'do not bother', and that is exactly what I argued.

Scored against PROSE it inverts. Over the FY2026 tabular corpus (25,160 notes + 46,881 code
descriptions, 21,249 distinct word-tokens):

    tokens matched, position 2 = \\w             6,333
    tokens matched, position 2 = digit|QA        5,907
    ELIMINATED                                     426

and the 426 are exactly what he predicted: ABC, ABL, ABO, ADA, AGL, ALK, ALL, ALT, AML, ANC, APL,
ARC, ARE, ASC, AST, ATV, Abt, Acn, Age, Air, All ... ordinary words and clinical abbreviations.

## THE COST, MEASURED, AND WHY IT IS ACCEPTABLE

ONE false negative across all 100,035 ICD10CM concept_codes in Athena 20260625: NOD.X, 'No
diagnosis', concept_id 35209512, invalid_reason='D', valid 20070101-20091231. Retired for sixteen
years and never a live code.

That is the SAME CLASS this repo already excludes by documented note -- ICD9CM's 3 defect rows
(92, sd, XX0.00), ICD10's 351 'Invalid ICD10 Concept' placeholders, ICD10PCS's MTHU000001-3. So the
exclusion is consistent with existing practice, not a new licence.

## WHAT SURVIVES OF THE HEADROOM ARGUMENT -- less than I claimed

The defence was: keep position 2 permissive so the next QA0-shaped surprise needs no edit. It is not
worthless, but it was overweighted, for two reasons.

1. It is speculation priced against a MEASURED cost. 426 tokens now, versus an unknown-probability
   future shape.
2. The trellis in vfm-ges already answers it: the durable protection is THE GUARD, not the slack.
   A harness that re-validates every row against the published vocabulary turns the next new shape
   into a red build rather than a silent false negative -- which is precisely the argument I made
   for tightening a consumer's grammar the same day, and then declined to apply here. That guard is
   bead vocabulary_formats-h0x and it does not exist yet; until it does, tightening carries real
   risk, and that is a SEQUENCING point, not a reason to stay loose forever.

Enumerating 'QA' rather than generalising to any-letter is the same discipline: FY2025 had 1,917
ICD-10-CM categories and ZERO with a letter in position 2; FY2026 has 1,918 and exactly one.

## SCOPE -- the other rows have not been re-scored this way

Only ICD10CM and ICD10CM.DOTLESS were narrowed. Every other row in the table was scored against
sibling vocabularies only and should be re-scored against prose. Filed separately.
