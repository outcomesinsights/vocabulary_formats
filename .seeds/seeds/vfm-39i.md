RULED 2026-10-31 (Ryan, verbatim): "for ndcalphanum, keep all 36". All 36 non-numeric NDC codes stay in NDC.ALPHANUM's target: the 12 legacy Mallinckrodt NDCs and 24 codes from labelers outside the original-system permission (corrected 2026-10-31: first written as "the 27 that were never NDCs", which was wrong in count and in claim). Only the three lower-case oxygen codes stay excluded. NDC.ALPHANUM is not retired.
